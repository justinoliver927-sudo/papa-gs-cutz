PAYMENTS & REFUNDS

Payment details.

Online payment is intentionally not enabled in this starter build.

No online payments are active. The website does not collect card or bank details and does not create payment transactions. Do not enter payment information into this website.

Current payment process

Confirm the service, price and accepted payment method directly with PaPa Gs Cutz before paying. A booking request is not a payment request and does not confirm an appointment.

Future checkout

Before enabling payments, the business must choose a supported payment provider, complete its merchant onboarding, configure server-side payment verification, and publish clear terms for deposits, cancellations, refunds, receipts and failed payments. Payment secrets must never be placed in browser JavaScript or a public GitHub repository.

Refunds and disputes

No website payment refunds can currently be processed because online checkout is disabled. For an in-person payment or a future payment arrangement, contact the business directly and retain any receipt. Consumer rights under applicable South African law remain unaffected.
