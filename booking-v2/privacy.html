PRIVACY NOTICE

How information is handled.

This notice explains the personal information this booking website may collect and why.

Starter template: before launch, confirm the business's actual data handling, retention periods, contact details and any cross-border processing with a South African privacy professional. This page is not a legal compliance certification.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the site, this can include your name, email address, phone number, preferred service, requested appointment date and time, notes you choose to provide, booking status, account identifiers and records of your agreement to the booking terms.

Why information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, manage and confirm booking requests, operate customer accounts, protect the website, and meet legitimate business or legal obligations. Optional promotional messages should only be sent where the appropriate consent has been given.

Where information is processed

Booking and account data may be stored in the configured Supabase database. If you choose to contact the business through WhatsApp or open Google Maps, those services receive information under their own policies. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information needed to deliver the website.

Access and security

Access should be restricted to people who need information to operate the business. The database is designed to use row-level security. No online service can promise absolute security; do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary sensitive information through booking notes.

Retention and your choices

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for booking administration, business records and applicable legal requirements, then securely deleted or de-identified. You can contact the business to ask about access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable law and recordkeeping duties.

Children

Where a booking concerns a child, a parent or guardian should make or approve the booking and provide only the information needed to arrange the service.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact PaPa Gs Cutz through WhatsApp. The business should replace this contact method with a monitored privacy contact if one is established.
