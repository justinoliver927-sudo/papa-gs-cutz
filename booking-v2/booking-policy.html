APPOINTMENTS

Booking policy.

Clear expectations before you request a time.

Requesting a booking

Complete the booking form with your contact details, requested service, preferred date and preferred time. The request may be stored in the business database if the site is connected. It will also open WhatsApp so you can send the details to the barber.

Confirmation

A submitted form or WhatsApp message does not guarantee an appointment. The barber must confirm the requested date, time, service and price. If the selected time is unavailable, you may be offered another time.

Changes and cancellations

Use WhatsApp to request changes or cancel as early as possible. The business should communicate any cancellation, late-arrival or no-show rules directly before confirming the appointment.

Prices

Prices are shown in South African rand and may change. Confirm the final price before the cut begins, especially for styles not listed on the service page.

Children

A parent or guardian should arrange or approve bookings for younger children and provide a suitable contact number.
